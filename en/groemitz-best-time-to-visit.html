Best time to visit Grömitz: climate and sea temperature month by month

Patricia Kreutzheide · Updated 23 August 2026 · 10 min read
Diesen Artikel auf Deutsch lesen

In short

For swimming, July to September is the best time in Grömitz: the Baltic reaches 18 °C in July and 19 °C in August, with air temperatures around 23 °C. For quiet and lower prices, come in May, June or September – typically 18 to 21 °C air and 11 to 16 °C water, with more daily sunshine than August.

The short answer

If swimming matters most: July to early September. If you want long, bright days at pleasant temperatures without the crowds: late May to late June. If quiet, price and clear air are the priority: September and October. And if you like storms, empty beaches and saunas: January and February – there are people who do, and we are among them.

The annual mean sea temperature is about 10 °C. Note the lag: in September the water is still 16 °C – as warm as June – while the air is already four degrees cooler. That is the main reason September is so popular with repeat guests here.

When can you swim in Grömitz?

As a rough rule: from 16 °C most guests go in without hesitating, and from 18 °C it feels comfortable to most people. Which gives:

  • June to September – the swimming season proper (16 to 19 °C)
  • May and October – for the hardy and for quick dips (11 to 13 °C)
  • November to April – winter bathing, which has a real following in Grömitz

One caveat: the Baltic responds faster than an ocean. Several days of offshore easterly wind can push the warm surface layer away and draw cooler water in – a 19-degree August can briefly sit at 15. Two calm days and it is back.

Month by month: what to expect

January and February are quiet. The beach is yours, the Grömitzer Welle pool and the saunas are running, and events such as the February beach fire punctuate the month. Cold, often grey – but a clear frosty day over the bay is spectacular.

March and April bring the first long walks. From April sunshine rises sharply to five hours a day, and the Easter holidays fill the resort for a fortnight.

May is one of the underrated months: 18 °C, six hours of sun, everything open, but not yet busy. The best time of year for cycling and walking.

June has the longest days – in mid-June it does not go properly dark until around 10pm. The water is 16 °C and the German school holidays have not started.

July and August are peak season: warmest water, full beach sections, the complete events programme including Sonne, Strand & Sterne in early July and Ostsee in Flammen in August.

September is our own favourite: 16 °C water, 19 °C air, quiet beaches, good light. The shoulder season starts on 5 September, so prices drop too.

October means storm walks, amber hunting and the Lichtermeer light festival at the end of the month. November and December are the quietest weeks, apart from Christmas, which counts as high season again.

Four Baltic weather rules that actually help

  1. The southern aspect is your friend. Grömitz faces south across the Bay of Lübeck, so in the prevailing westerlies and north-westerlies the resort sits in the lee of the land.
  2. Easterly wind cools the water. Two or three days of firm easterly can knock several degrees off the sea temperature.
  3. Rain days are rarely rainy days. The ten statistical rain days in July usually mean 20-minute showers, not all-day rain. For the exceptions there are indoor plans.
  4. Evenings are cooler than the day suggests. Temperatures drop quickly by the water after sunset. A light jacket belongs in every summer day-pack.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Grömitz?

For swimming, July to early September: 18 to 19 °C sea temperature and around 23 °C air. For quieter days in mild weather, May, June and September work well.

How warm is the Baltic Sea at Grömitz in summer?

Around 18 °C in July and 19 °C in August. June and September both average about 16 °C. A sustained easterly wind can drop the temperature by several degrees for a few days.

Is September still warm enough to swim in Grömitz?

Yes. At around 16 °C the water in September is as warm as in June, with air temperatures near 19 °C. The shoulder season starts on 5 September, so nightly rates are noticeably lower too.

How many rainy days does Grömitz have?

Statistically eight to eleven per month, with ten in both July and August. These are usually short showers rather than all-day rain; July averages eight hours of sunshine a day.

When is low season in Grömitz?

At the Lieblingsplatz Grömitz apartment, low season in 2026 runs 4 January to 28 March and 31 October to 19 December, at €99 per night. The visitor tax also drops to €2.50 per day in that period.

Is Grömitz worth visiting in winter?

For guests who want empty beaches, storm walks and wellness, yes. The Grömitzer Welle seawater pool and saunas are open all year, and low-season rates are the lowest of the year.
