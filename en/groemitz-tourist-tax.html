Visitor tax and the ostseecard in Grömitz 2026: cost, benefits, billing

Patricia Kreutzheide · Updated 23 August 2026 · 7 min read
Diesen Artikel auf Deutsch lesen

In short

Grömitz collects its visitor tax through the ostseecard. In 2026 it costs €4.00 per person per day in high season and €2.50 in low season for Grömitz and Lensterstrand. It covers free access to the 8 km bathing beach, reduced bus fares, cheaper entry to the Grömitzer Welle pool and free guided walks, among other things.

What is the ostseecard?

The ostseecard is the form in which Grömitz collects its visitor tax. It is not an optional discount card but a municipal levy – and at the same time an access and benefits card for a range of facilities.

The municipality's reasoning: the revenue funds things a town of around 7,500 residents would not otherwise have – beach maintenance and lifeguards, the promenade, the spa park, the events programme and sports provision.

What does it include?

  • Free access to the licensed bathing beach – eight kilometres, lifeguarded in high season
  • Reduced bus fares along the coast between Grömitz, Kellenhusen, Dahme and Neustadt in Holstein
  • Cheaper entry to the Grömitzer Welle, the seawater leisure pool and sauna complex
  • Free or reduced entry to events
  • Free participation in guided walks and the sports programme
  • Library access
  • Discounts at bike hire shops and other partner businesses

The sports and walking programme is the underrated part: in season there is something free almost every day, from Nordic walking and beach yoga to guided coastal hikes.

How is the ostseecard billed?

With us it is straightforward: we register your stay and issue the cards, charged per person per night. You pay the visitor tax with the final invoice – it is not included in the nightly rate of €99 / €138 / €158, any more than the €110 final cleaning is.

On transparencyWe always show the ostseecard as a separate line rather than folding it into the nightly rate. It makes comparison with booking portals fairer – there the levy often only appears at the last step.

Common misunderstandings

“I am not going to the beach, so I do not pay.” The visitor tax is a levy on staying in the area, not a beach entry fee. It applies regardless of use.

“Children always pay full price.” The municipal by-law provides for reductions and exemptions for children and certain groups. The current Grömitz by-law is what counts – tell us if you are travelling with children and we will clarify in advance.

“The card is valid all along the Baltic.” It is not. The ostseecard applies within its collection area; the bus discount covers the coastal stretch of Grömitz, Kellenhusen, Dahme and Neustadt in Holstein.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the tourist tax in Grömitz in 2026?

In Grömitz and Lensterstrand the ostseecard costs €4.00 per person per day in high season and €2.50 in low season. In the other villages of the municipality it is €2.50 and €1.80 respectively.

What does the ostseecard include?

Free access to the eight-kilometre licensed bathing beach, reduced coastal bus fares, cheaper entry to the Grömitzer Welle pool, free guided walks and sports sessions, library access and discounts at bike hire shops.

When is high season for the Grömitz visitor tax?

High season runs 1 April to 31 October and 25 December to 3 January. Low season is 4 January to 31 March and 1 November to 24 December.

Is the tourist tax included in the nightly rate?

No. At the Lieblingsplatz Grömitz apartment the ostseecard is billed separately per person per night and shown as its own line on the final invoice.

Do children pay tourist tax in Grömitz?

The Grömitz by-law provides reductions and exemptions for children and certain groups. The by-law in force at the time of your stay applies; we are happy to clarify before you arrive.

Is there an annual ostseecard?

Yes. For Grömitz and Lensterstrand the annual card costs €112.00, and €70.00 for the other villages in the municipality (2026 rates).
