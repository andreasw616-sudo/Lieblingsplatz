Grömitz in winter and out of season: what actually works

Patricia Kreutzheide · Updated 23 August 2026 · 9 min read
Diesen Artikel auf Deutsch lesen

In short

Between November and March a night in Grömitz costs far less – €99 instead of €158 with us – and the visitor tax drops to €2.50 a day. The Grömitzer Welle seawater pool and saunas stay open, as does the diving gondola (Wed–Sat 11am–4pm) and part of the restaurant scene. The eight-kilometre beach is more or less yours.

Why the Baltic in winter at all?

Because it is the only time of year when you have eight kilometres of beach and barely meet anyone on it. In January an hour's walk on the sand might bring you past five people, three of them with dogs. And the light on the coast in winter is low and clear, the sunsets last longer, and the air is clean enough that after two days you sleep differently.

If you want bustle, warm water and a full events programme, come in July. If you want quiet, saunas, good food and long walks, come in January – and pay considerably less for it.

What does the off season cost?

The price difference is substantial. At Lieblingsplatz the low season – 4 January to 28 March and 31 October to 19 December – costs €99 a night instead of €158. In parallel the visitor tax drops to €2.50 per person per day from 1 November.

A winter weekend, in practice

Friday: arrive in the afternoon, shop in town, cook in the evening. If the sky is clear, watch the sunset from the roof terrace – in December that is already around 4pm.

Saturday: a beach walk north towards the cliffs in the morning, then hot chocolate. Sauna at the Grömitzer Welle in the afternoon with a view over the bay. Fish for dinner on the promenade.

Sunday: Cismar Abbey and its surroundings, then a last walk along the pier. If you fancy it: winter bathing has a real following here, and it is not a bad idea followed by a sauna.

Events outside the season

  • Strandfeuer – mid-February, beach fires, mulled wine and a torchlit walk.
  • Romance the Show – a circus programme running about ten days in February.
  • Crossdog on the Beach – late February, a canicross running event on the beach.
  • Jugend rockt Grömitz – April, a festival for new bands in the spa park.
  • Lichtermeer – late October, illuminated light installations across the resort.
  • Cismar Martinmas market – early to mid-November around St Martin's Day.

Dates change; the current listing is at groemitz.de.

Packing list for a Baltic winter

  1. A windproof jacket – more important than heavy insulation. Wind is the issue, not temperature.
  2. Hat and gloves – even at 5 °C, the moment you stand by the water.
  3. Sturdy shoes with grip – wet sand and steps on the cliff path.
  4. Swimwear and a sauna towel – for the Grömitzer Welle.
  5. A head torch – it is dark by 4:30pm in December, which makes early-evening beach walks easier.
  6. You will not need a good reading lamp – but you will need books.

Frequently asked questions

Is Grömitz worth visiting in winter?

For guests who want quiet, empty beaches, storm walks and wellness, yes. The Grömitzer Welle seawater pool and saunas are open all year, and at €99 a night the rates are the lowest of the year.

What is open in Grömitz in winter?

The beach, promenade, pier and the Grömitzer Welle are open all year. The diving gondola runs Wednesday to Saturday, 11am–4pm, from November to March. Some restaurants stay open throughout; smaller cafés and bike hire operate on reduced hours.

When is low season in Grömitz?

At Lieblingsplatz Grömitz, low season runs 4 January to 28 March and 31 October to 19 December. The period from 19 December to 3 January counts as high season.

How cold does it get in Grömitz in winter?

Daytime highs are around 3 °C in January and 4 °C in February, with overnight lows of −2 to −1 °C. Hard frost is rarer on the coast than inland; wind is the defining factor.

Can you swim in Grömitz in winter?

Winter bathing is popular in Grömitz, with sea temperatures between 2 and 5 °C. If you prefer it warm, the Grömitzer Welle seawater pool runs at 27 to 33 °C.

What events take place in Grömitz in winter?

Among others: the Strandfeuer beach fire in mid-February with a torchlit walk and mulled wine, the Romance the Show circus programme in February, the Crossdog on the Beach canicross event in late February and the Lichtermeer light festival in late October.
