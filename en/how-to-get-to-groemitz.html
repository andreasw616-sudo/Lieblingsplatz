Getting there

Getting to Grömitz: car, train, parking and EV charging

Leon Kreutzheide · Updated 23 August 2026 · 8 min read
Diesen Artikel auf Deutsch lesen

In short

By car, take the A1 to the Neustadt-Pelzerhaken/Grömitz exit, then about 10 km on the B501. Hamburg is roughly 110 km and 1 hour 20 minutes away, Lübeck about 45 km and 45 minutes. By rail, travel to Neustadt in Holstein and continue 20 minutes by bus or taxi. Grömitz has eight EV charging stations.

Driving to Grömitz

The standard route is simple: A1 towards Puttgarden/Fehmarn as far as the Neustadt-Pelzerhaken/Grömitz exit, then about 10 kilometres on the B501 straight into Grömitz. The B501 passes the Grömitzer Höhe at the edge of town; from there it is a few minutes to Seestraße.

Coming from Kiel and the north, take the B76 and B202 to Oldenburg in Holstein and then the B501 south. That way is prettier but slower.

Arrival day and traffic

The A1 between Hamburg and Lübeck is the bottleneck. Three things we have learned:

  • Saturday morning is the worst slot. Between 9am and 1pm arriving and departing traffic overlaps at almost every Baltic resort.
  • Friday afternoon out of Hamburg is also heavy. If you can flex, travel after 7pm on Friday or on Sunday morning.
  • If the A1 is blocked, the B75/B207 via Bad Schwartau is a usable alternative, though it costs time.

Our check-in timesThe apartment is ready from 3pm, departure by 10am. If you will arrive later, just let us know and we will work around it.

By train

Grömitz has no station of its own. The nearest is Neustadt in Holstein, about 20 minutes away. From Hamburg Hauptbahnhof you travel via Lübeck to Neustadt; the station café houses a NAH.SH service point (Mon–Fri 7am–3pm).

From Neustadt station:

  • Local bus towards Grömitz – the ostseecard gets you reduced fares along the coast.
  • Taxi – easiest with luggage, about 20 minutes.

Oldenburg in Holstein works as an alternative station, also served by buses. In summer there are additional night train connections from southern Europe via Hamburg.

By air

The nearest airports are Lübeck (LBC) and Hamburg Fuhlsbüttel (HAM). From Hamburg it is about 1 hour 20 minutes by hire car, or roughly 2 hours 30 by train via the main station and Neustadt.

Parking in Grömitz

Grömitz has both free and paid parking; spaces near the beach and promenade are chargeable in season. For holiday apartment guests this is usually a side issue, because a parking space comes with the accommodation – ours included: Lieblingsplatz has its own space at the building, so you park once and leave the car there for the week.

If you are driving in for the day, there is parking at the Grömitzer Höhe and around the marina; on hot summer days the beachside areas fill up by late morning.

Charging an electric car

Grömitz currently has eight public charging stations, with more planned. You can also charge at our building: we offer a flat charging fee of €15 for the whole stay, however much you draw. Mention it when you book so we can enable the connection.

Frequently asked questions

Which motorway exit do I take for Grömitz?

Take the A1 towards Puttgarden as far as the Neustadt-Pelzerhaken/Grömitz exit, then follow the B501 for about 10 kilometres into Grömitz.

How long is the drive from Hamburg to Grömitz?

About 110 kilometres and roughly 1 hour 20 minutes without traffic. Allow an extra hour on summer Saturdays.

What is the nearest railway station to Grömitz?

Neustadt in Holstein, around 20 minutes away, with local buses and taxis onward to Grömitz. Oldenburg in Holstein is an alternative.

Are there EV charging points in Grömitz?

Yes, Grömitz has eight public charging stations with more planned. Guests at the Lieblingsplatz Grömitz apartment can also charge at the building for a flat fee of €15 for the whole stay.

Does the apartment come with a parking space?

Yes. Lieblingsplatz Grömitz at Seestraße 9a has its own parking space at the building, so there is no need to use public parking near the beach.

Do I need a car in Grömitz?

Not for the resort itself: beach, promenade, pier, supermarket and restaurants are all walkable from Seestraße 9a. For trips into the surrounding area, a bicycle, bus or car is useful.
